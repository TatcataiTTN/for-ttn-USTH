Status and limits

  • Sources are only the lecture (120 slides) and the textbook; no sample exam, no syllabus: the coverage numbers refer to the papers written here.
  • Numbers were recomputed in Python with two independent methods (91.0/91 cross-checks passed); simple models (Mie spheres, package sphere) are illustrations, not data.
  • Values read from graphs are approximate and labelled; the module on the light field (G) is textbook-only because the lecture file has no slide on it.
  • Lecture and textbook differ in a few places (RTE factor 2π, pure-water β values, depolarisation); see Sources and limits.
  • Progress is saved in your browser only. Slide images belong to their authors; the textbook is CC BY 2.0 and its images are not copied.

Answer-position audit over 138 multiple-choice questions: A/B/C/D = 28% / 27% / 24% / 22%; correct answer is the longest option in 36% and the shortest in 15% of cases.

Sources: (1) the lecture Marine Optics by Hubert Loisel (LOG; file Optique-marine-English-UBATUBA, 120 slides) — slide numbers are cited as “slide N”; (2) the Ocean Optics Web Book (CC BY 2.0), cited by chapter title and URL; (3) home-made checks in Python.
