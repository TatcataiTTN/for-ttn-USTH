F1 · Radiative transfer equation (lecture slide 5)

\begin{aligned}\frac{d\sym{L}{L(\theta,\varphi)}}{\sym{r}{dr}}&=\underbrace{-\,\sym{c}{c}\,\sym{L}{L(\theta,\varphi)}}_{\footnotesize\text{loss: absorption + scattering out}}\\[0.15em]&\quad+\underbrace{\int_{4\pi}\sym{beta}{\beta(\theta,\varphi;\theta^{\prime},\varphi^{\prime})}\,\sym{L}{L(\theta^{\prime},\varphi^{\prime})}\,\sym{om}{d\omega^{\prime}}}_{\footnotesize\text{gain: scattering in from all directions}}\\[0.15em]&\quad+\underbrace{\sym{S}{S}}_{\footnotesize\text{internal sources}}\end{aligned}
\sym{c}{c}\;=\;\sym{a}{a}\;+\;\sym{b}{b}
SymbolNameMeaningWhat it impliesNumbers assigned
L(\theta,\varphi)radiance [W m⁻² sr⁻¹ nm⁻¹]Light power per unit area, solid angle and wavelength travelling in direction (θ, φ).The unknown of the equation; θ is measured from the vertical.
drpath element [m]Thin slab of water crossed by the beam.Everything is a rate per metre along the path.
cattenuation coefficient [m⁻¹]Fraction of the beam lost per metre, by absorption and by scattering out of the beam: c = a + b.Beer–Lambert uses c, not a: a transmissometer measures c.example used in module A: a = 0.08, b = 0.12 ⇒ c = 0.20 m⁻¹ (illustrative)
example used in module E: c = 0.5 m⁻¹ ⇒ 61 % left after 1 m (illustrative)
aabsorption coefficient [m⁻¹]Photon energy converted to heat or chemistry.Removes photons for good; sets light penetration and the colour of the sea.pure water minimum: ≈ 0.005 m⁻¹ near 415 nm (slide 24, read from graph)
clearest gyre, total: ≈ 0.007 m⁻¹ at 410–420 nm (slide 26, Morel et al. 2007)
bscattering coefficient [m⁻¹]Photons change direction.Redirects, never destroys: scattered photons can re-enter the beam from other directions.pure sea water, 500 nm, S = 37: 2.88e-03 m⁻¹ (verified.json (slide-20 formula))
Case 1 particles, 400 nm: ≈ 0.18 m⁻¹ (slide 58, read from graph)
\betavolume scattering function [m⁻¹ sr⁻¹]How scattered power is distributed over angles (θ,φ) ← (θ′,φ′).Forward-peaked for particles, symmetric for pure water; b = 2π∫β sinθ dθ.pure water, 90°, 546 nm, S = 0: 9.44×10⁻⁵ m⁻¹ sr⁻¹ (slide-20 formula; textbook 0.869e-4)
d\omega^{\prime}solid-angle element [sr]Integration over every incoming direction; dω = sinθ dθ dφ.The slide prints a factor 2π in front of the integral; the textbook does not.
Ssource term [W m⁻³ sr⁻¹ nm⁻¹]Light created inside the water (fluorescence, Raman, bioluminescence).Zero for purely elastic problems; matters for Chl fluorescence.
Ý nghĩa: ánh sáng mất đi do hấp thụ và tán xạ ra khỏi chùm (c = a + b), được bù do tán xạ từ hướng khác vào, cộng nguồn nội tại S. Slide 5 in thêm thừa số 2π trước tích phân; textbook không có.
