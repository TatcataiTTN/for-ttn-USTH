Practice calculators

Ten tools that run in your browser (no server). Every formula comes from the lecture or the textbook (named in each box); results are tested automatically against Python — see tests/test_calc.js and data/verified.json.

1 · Beer–Lambert and attenuation (modules A, E)

T = e−cL; c = −ln T/L (slide 5 without gain terms). Defaults: c = 1 m⁻¹, L = 0.25 m (illustrative path).

2 · Pure-water VSF and bw (module B)

βw = A(λ/500)e(1 + 0.3S/37)·10⁻⁴[1 + cos²θ(1−δ)/(1+δ)] (slides 20, 23); bw = 2πβ(90°)(2 + 2k/3).

3 · CDOM / NAP exponential absorption (module C)

a(λ) = a(λ₀)exp[−S(λ−λ₀)]. CDOM S′ ≈ 0.014, NAP S ≈ 0.011 nm⁻¹ (slides 35, 41).

4 · Package effect Q*a (module C)

Q*a(ρ) = (3/2)[1 + 2e−ρ/ρ + 2(e−ρ−1)/ρ²]/ρ for a homogeneous sphere (Morel & Bricaud 1981 form; ρ = intracellular absorption × diameter, illustrative). Concept: slide 33.

5 · Particle scattering power law (module D)

bp(λ) = bp(550)(λ/550)n (slide 49). n = 0, −1, −3.

6 · IOP = Σ N·Q·S (modules F)

c = (N/V)·Q·πr² for one population (slide 63).

7 · bp from Chl (module F)

bp(550) = 0.30·Chl0.7 (slide 65, Loisel & Morel 1998; Case-1 regression).

8 · Euphotic and Secchi depth (module G, textbook)

z1% = 4.605/Kd; zSD = Γ/(Kd + c), Γ ≈ 6–9 (Textbook: K functions; The Secchi Disk).

9 · POC/SPM from particle absorption ratio (module F)

POC/SPM = 0.0133·exp(1.27 R), R = ap(675)/ap(570) (slide 74).

10 · CDOM–salinity conservative mixing (module F)

a(S) = afresh(1 − S/36) + aoceanS/36 (idea of slide 77; illustrative end members).

Sources: (1) the lecture Marine Optics by Hubert Loisel (LOG; file Optique-marine-English-UBATUBA, 120 slides) — slide numbers are cited as “slide N”; (2) the Ocean Optics Web Book (CC BY 2.0), cited by chapter title and URL; (3) home-made checks in Python.
