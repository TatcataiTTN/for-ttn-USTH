G · Light field (textbook)
AOPs, K_d, reflectance, Secchi depth, IOP–AOP link: NOT in the lecture file, from the textbook · Textbook (oceanopticsbook.info)
Guiding question: how do IOPs become the light field and the signal a satellite sees? (textbook, not in the lecture file)
Source labelThe lecture outline (slide 9) lists “Link between IOPs and the light field”, but the 120-slide file contains no slide on it. This module is built from the Ocean Optics Web Book (oceanopticsbook.info, CC BY 2.0) and is labelled as textbook content throughout.

AOPs are the radiometric quantities that describe the light field in the water (K_d, reflectance). They depend on the IOPs and on the Sun and sky.

Contents: IOP versus AOP · K_d, reflectance and penetration · Secchi depth · Worked example, Q&A, memory hooks · Exam checklist · Slides · Quiz

IOP versus AOP

Apparent optical properties depend both on the medium (the IOPs) and on the angular structure of the radiance, and are stable enough to describe a water body. Ed alone is not stable: a cloud can change it by an order of magnitude within seconds (textbook, Apparent Optical Properties).

K_d, reflectance and penetration

The diffuse attenuation coefficient and the profile of downwelling irradiance (textbook, K functions):

TextbookKd ≡ −d ln Ed/dz;   Ed(z) = Ed(0) exp(−Kd z);   a ≤ Kd μ̄d ≤ c

Reflectance: R ≡ Eu/Ed; remote-sensing reflectance Rrs ≡ Lw/Ed (sr⁻¹) (textbook, Reflectances). Qualitatively R ≈ f bb/(a + bb) (classical result of Gordon et al., 1975; not found on the pages we crawled).

Numbers (Python). 1 % light depth = 4.605/Kd: 154, 115, 46, 15.4 m for Kd = 0.03, 0.04, 0.1, 0.3 m⁻¹.

Secchi depth

Classic theory: zSD = Γ/(⟨Kdv⟩ + ⟨cv⟩), Γ ≈ 6–9 (8 in HydroLight). Lee et al. (2015): zSD = γ/(2.5 Kd(λmin)), usable from satellite imagery (textbook, The Secchi Disk). Example: Kd = 0.1, c = 0.2 m⁻¹ ⇒ 26.7 m.

Worked example, quick Q&A and memory hooks

Worked example · Clear versus turbid

Clear ocean Kd = 0.04 m⁻¹: 1 % light at 115 m; coastal Kd = 0.3 m⁻¹: 15 m. Same formula, 7.5× different depth.

Quick Q&A

Q: Is K_d an IOP?
A: No, it depends on the Sun angle (textbook).
Q: Which wavelength matters for Secchi depth in the Lee model?
A: The wavelength of minimum K_d.

Memory hooks

  • IOP = water; AOP = water + light.IOP = nước; AOP = nước + ánh sáng.

Key points in two lines
  • AOP phụ thuộc cả môi trường lẫn trường sáng; K_d = −d ln E_d/dz.
  • Độ sâu 1 % = 4,6/K_d; Secchi = Γ/(K_d + c), Γ ≈ 8.
