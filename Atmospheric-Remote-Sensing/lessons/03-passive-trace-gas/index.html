M3 · Trace-gas retrieval
Passive remote sensing of trace gases · Study guide chapter 3

  • Where is the information on the vertical distribution of the gas coming from?

The measured quantity

A passive sensor does not emit light; it only records the spectral radiance I(λ) (W m⁻² sr⁻¹ µm⁻¹) arriving from one viewing direction, in many wavelength channels (a spectrum). Gas concentration is never measured directly.

Spectral rangeQuantityInstruments
UV – visible – SWIR (backscattered sunlight)Radiance normalised to reflectance R(λ) = π I / (μ₀ E₀)GOME-2, OMI, TROPOMI, OCO-2
Thermal infraredBrightness temperature T_BIASI, AIRS, CrIS
OccultationTransmission I/I₀ACE-FTS, SAGE
Forward modely = F(x, b) + ε

A retrieval inverts the radiative transfer model F to find the state x (gas amount) from the measured spectrum y, while b (temperature, surface, clouds, aerosols) are other parameters.

(i) Total column: DOAS

The vertical column is VCD = ∫ n(z) dz (molecule cm⁻²; O₃ uses Dobson Units, 1 DU = 2.69×10¹⁶ molecule cm⁻²). The standard UV–visible method is DOAS (Differential Optical Absorption Spectroscopy), in three steps:

  1. Spectral fit → slant column (SCD). From Beer–Lambert, the optical depth is split into a rapidly varying part (molecular absorption structures) and a smooth part (Rayleigh, Mie, surface).
  2. Air mass factor (AMF). A radiative transfer model computes AMF = SCD / VCD, depending on geometry, albedo, clouds, aerosols and the a priori profile shape.
  3. Stratosphere–troposphere separation (for tropospheric gases): reference sector over a clean ocean, or chemistry-transport model assimilation.
Spectral fitln( I0(λ) / I(λ) ) = Σi σi′(λ) · SCDi + Σp ap λp  ;  SCD = ∫ n ds
Tropospheric columnVCD = (SCD − SCDstrat) / AMFtrop  ;  AMF ≈ 1/cos θ0 + 1/cos θ (geometric approximation)
Example · AMF and VCD (numbers checked in Python)
  1. θ₀ = 60°, θ = 30° → AMF = 2 + 1.1547 = 3.1547
  2. SCD = 8×10¹⁵; SCDstrat = 3×10¹⁵ molecule cm⁻²
  3. VCD = 5×10¹⁵ / 3.1547 = 1.585e+15 molecule cm⁻²

Alternative approaches: optimal estimation with scaling of an a priori profile (CO in SWIR/TIR), or ratio methods for greenhouse gases (XCO₂ = CO₂ column / dry-air column, using the O₂ A-band).

(ii) Vertical profile: optimal estimation

Divide the atmosphere into N layers, state x = (x₁…x_N). Linearise around a reference:

Linearisationy = y0 + K (x − x0) + ε  ;  K = ∂F/∂x (Jacobian, weighting functions)

The problem is ill-posed: more unknowns than independent pieces of information, plus noise — so it must be regularised. The standard method is optimal estimation (Rodgers):

Cost functionχ² = (y − F(x))T Sε−1 (y − F(x)) + (x − xa)T Sa−1 (x − xa)
Linear solutionx̂ = xa + (KT Sε−1 K + Sa−1)−1 KT Sε−1 (y − K xa)

xa: a priori profile; Sa: its covariance; Sε: measurement-noise covariance. If nonlinear, iterate with Gauss–Newton. Other methods: Tikhonov regularisation, onion peeling for limb data.

Where does the vertical information come from?

From the fact that different measurements are sensitive to different altitudes, i.e. their weighting functions K(z) peak at different heights:

Source of informationHow it worksExample
Absorption strength vs λ (nadir)Strong absorption → photons do not penetrate deep → signal from high altitudes; weak absorption → from lower layersO₃ in the UV: 270 nm sees the upper stratosphere; 310–330 nm reaches the troposphere (BUV/SBUV)
Pressure and temperature dependence of line shapes (TIR)Line width ∝ pressure: line centre probes high, wings probe low; T(z) creates emission contrastIASI O₃/CO profiles
Viewing geometry (limb, occultation)Each line of sight has a tangent height; scanning the limb gives 1–3 km vertical resolutionOnion peeling
OtherTemperature-dependent cross-sections, multiple angles, Raman/Rayleigh scatteringA little extra information

Exam checklist — points you must mention

  • Measured quantity = spectral radiance (reflectance / brightness temperature / transmission), NOT concentration.
  • Column: DOAS fit → SCD; AMF from RT model → VCD = SCD/AMF.
  • Profile: forward model + Jacobian K + Optimal Estimation with a priori.
  • Vertical information: different penetration depths vs λ, pressure broadening of lines, temperature contrast, limb geometry.
Key points (Vietnamese summary from the guide)
  • Cảm biến thụ động đo bức xạ phổ (radiance) — không đo nồng độ trực tiếp; nồng độ được “truy hồi” bằng cách nghịch đảo mô hình truyền bức xạ.
  • Cột tổng: DOAS → cột nghiêng SCD; hệ số khối khí AMF → cột thẳng đứng VCD = SCD/AMF.
  • Profil thẳng đứng: ước lượng tối ưu (Optimal Estimation) dùng ma trận Jacobian K và profil tiên nghiệm.
  • Thông tin độ cao đến từ: độ hấp thụ mạnh/yếu theo bước sóng, mở rộng vạch theo áp suất, tương phản nhiệt độ, và hình học quan sát limb.
