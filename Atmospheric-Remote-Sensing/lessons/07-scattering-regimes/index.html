Big or small compared with the wavelength?

How light scatters depends on the particle size compared with the wavelength. Air molecules are far smaller than the wavelength; aerosol particles are comparable to it; raindrops are thousands of times larger. Two numbers decide the regime: the size parameter and the complex refractive index m = n + ik.

Rayleigh: molecules and very fine particles

A particle much smaller than the wavelength behaves as an oscillating dipole in a uniform field.

Cross-section and phase functionσs = (8π/3) k⁴ r⁶ |(m² − 1)/(m² + 2)|² ∝ r⁶ / λ⁴  ;  P(Θ) = ¾ (1 + cos²Θ)
  • Strong λ⁻⁴ dependence → blue sky, red sunsets.
  • Forward and backward scattering are symmetric; light scattered at 90° is fully polarised.
Example · numbers
  • Double the radius (λ fixed): σ increases 2⁶ = 64 times.
  • Halve the wavelength: σ increases 2⁴ = 16 times.
  • 450 nm compared with 650 nm: (650/450)⁴ = 4.35 times.

Mie and geometric optics

Mie (x ≈ 0.1–50) is the exact solution of Maxwell’s equations for a homogeneous sphere, as an infinite series of multipoles. The extinction efficiency Qext = σext/(πr²) oscillates with x and tends to 2 for large x (“extinction paradox”). The weak wavelength dependence makes haze and clouds white/grey; the forward peak is strong (g ≈ 0.6–0.8) and the angular structure complex (glory, rainbow). Non-spherical particles (dust, ice) need T-matrix or discrete-dipole methods.

Geometric optics (x ≫ 1): light is treated as rays (Snell, Fresnel) plus diffraction. Qext → 2 (half diffraction, half reflection/refraction), independent of wavelength, so clouds are white; phenomena: rainbow 42°, halo 22° around hexagonal ice crystals; very strong forward diffraction peak.

Coarse aerosol in visible light

Mineral dust and sea salt have r ≈ 1–10 µm; at λ ≈ 0.55 µm:

Size parameterx = 2π × (1 to 10 µm) / 0.55 µm ≈ 11 to 114

→ the upper part of the Mie regime, approaching geometric optics (Mie still applies; geometric optics is a good approximation for the largest particles). Consequences: AOD nearly independent of wavelength (α ≈ 0–0.5), strong forward scattering, Qext ≈ 2. Dust is non-spherical, so Mie for spheres is only an approximation.

Molecules (Rayleigh, ∝ λ⁻⁴) scatter blue more than red. Cloud droplets (x ≫ 1) scatter all colours equally (Qext ≈ 2), so clouds are white.

Worked example, quick Q&A and memory hooks

Worked example · the same particle in different regimes
  1. Cloud droplet r = 10 µm: visible (λ = 0.55 µm) → x ≈ 114 → geometric optics (white clouds).
  2. Same droplet seen by a cloud radar (λ = 3 mm) → x ≈ 0.02 → Rayleigh → radar reflectivity ∝ r⁶ (∝ D⁶).
  3. Lesson: the regime depends on the ratio r/λ, not on the particle alone.

Quick Q&A

Q: Why are sunsets red?
A: The long slant path removes blue light by Rayleigh scattering (λ⁻⁴).
Q: Why is polluted haze white-grey?
A: Mie scattering by fine particles depends weakly on λ.
Q: What is the extinction paradox?
A: For x ≫ 1, Q_ext → 2: a large particle removes twice its geometric cross-section (diffraction + geometric part).
Q: Can Mie theory be used for dust?
A: Only approximately: dust is non-spherical → spheroids or T-matrix.

Memory hooks

  • Ocean waves meeting an obstacle: a small bamboo pole (Rayleigh) is passed almost unchanged; a rock the size of a wavelength (Mie) creates complex interference; a long dyke (geometric optics) casts a clear “shadow” behind it.Sóng biển gặp vật cản.

Exam checklist — points you must mention

  • Size parameter x = 2πr/λ (+ refractive index m).
  • Rayleigh x ≪ 1 (molecules), λ⁻⁴, symmetric P(Θ).
  • Mie x ~ 1 (fine aerosols), Lorenz–Mie theory, forward peak, Q_ext oscillates → 2.
  • Geometric optics x ≫ 1 (raindrops, ice), wavelength-independent.
  • Coarse aerosol in visible: x ~ 10–100 → Mie (large-particle limit, near geometric optics), α ≈ 0.
Key points (Vietnamese summary from the guide)
  • Chế độ tán xạ phụ thuộc thông số kích thước x = 2πr/λ.
  • Rayleigh (x ≪ 1): phân tử khí, ∝ λ⁻⁴, trời xanh. Mie (x ≈ 1): aerosol mịn, tán xạ mạnh về phía trước. Quang hình (x ≫ 1): giọt mưa, tinh thể băng, không phụ thuộc bước sóng.
  • Aerosol thô (bụi, muối biển, r ≈ 1–10 µm) với ánh sáng khả kiến: x ≈ 10–100 → chế độ Mie (phần trên, tiến gần quang hình), số mũ Ångström ≈ 0.
