Suggested study path

  1. Read a lesson (plain-language part first, then formulas and worked examples) and watch its slides.
  2. Take the quick quiz at the bottom, then open the question bank; filter by “Original” to do the mock-exam questions first.
  3. Practise the numbers in the calculators with the values from the mock exam.
  4. Written practice: draft the 8 questions of the mock exam in 120 minutes, then compare with the suggested answers and self-grade.
  5. Finish with a 30-minute multiple-choice set; for every mistake, go back to the matching module.

Status and limits (honest notes)

  • Single source: the study pack supplied by the student (25-page guide, mock exam, figures, plotting code). No textbook came with it, so the site cites no book page numbers and adds no knowledge beyond the guide, except calculations explicitly labelled as home-made and computed in Python.
  • Numbers: every calculated value is recomputed in Python (numpy/scipy) by two independent methods; the JavaScript calculators are compared with Python in 455 checks. The simple models (TIR brightness-temperature depression, O₂ above the layer, the 12-level averaging-kernel simulation) are illustrations, not measured data.
  • Questions: “Original” questions come from the mock exam, the exam checklists and the guide’s own content (each has its source); “Added” questions are extra items based on the guide. Answer positions and lengths were audited for bias.
  • Language: English by default, as the course is taught in English. Each lesson also keeps the short Vietnamese summary from the study guide. No Chinese version.
  • Figures: the 18 figures and 4 lecture photos come from the original pack; wide ones are scaled down on slides, so text inside a figure can be smaller than 20 px (all HTML slide text is ≥ 20 px).
  • Progress is stored in your browser’s localStorage (lost if you clear site data or use a private window).
