The regime is set by x = 2πr/λ and the complex refractive index m = n + ik.

Rayleigh: molecules and very fine particles

  • Double the radius → 64 times (26)
  • Halve λ → 16 times (24)
  • Blue sky, red sunsets

Mie: particles of wavelength size

  • Maxwell solution for a sphere (Lorenz–Mie)
  • Qext oscillates with x and tends to 2
  • Weak λ dependence (Ångström α ≈ 0–2)
  • Forward peak: g ≈ 0.6–0.8
  • Non-spherical: T-matrix

Coarse aerosol in visible light

λ = 0.55 µm:   r = 1 µm → x ≈ 11  ;  r = 10 µm → x ≈ 114
  • Upper Mie regime, approaching geometric optics
  • AOD almost independent of λ: α ≈ 0–0.5
  • Strong forward scattering; Qext ≈ 2
  • Dust is non-spherical: spheroids, T-matrix

  • Rayleigh: λ−4 → blue sky
  • Mie: fine aerosols, forward peak
  • Coarse aerosol: x ≈ 10–100, α ≈ 0
