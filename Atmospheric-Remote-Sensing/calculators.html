Practice calculators

Nine tools that run in your browser (no server). All formulas come from the study guide; the results are tested automatically against Python (455 comparisons, including the averaging-kernel matrices) — see tests/test_calc.js and the notebook.

1 · Lifetime and parallel sinks (Ch. 1)

τchem = 1/(k·[OH]); parallel sinks 1/τ = Σ 1/τi. Defaults are the CO + OH example of the mock exam.

2 · Lifetime from a plume (Ch. 1)

C(x) ∝ exp(−x/(U·τ)) ⇒ τ = L/U, where L is the observed e-folding length.

3 · Beer–Lambert and zenith angle (Ch. 2)

τslant = τvertical/cos θ; T = e−τ. Defaults: mock exam Q2(c).

4 · Planck, Wien, Stefan–Boltzmann (Ch. 2)

Try 5778 K (Sun) and 288 K (Earth): λmax is about 0.50 µm and 10.06 µm.

5 · DOAS: AMF, VCD and Dobson Units (Ch. 3)

AMF ≈ 1/cos θ₀ + 1/cos θ; VCD = (SCD − SCDstrat)/AMF. Enter SCD in units of 10¹⁵ molecule cm⁻².

6 · Averaging kernel and DOFS (Ch. 5)

12-level model: K = Gaussians of width w levels; Sε = σε²I; a-priori correlated with height. A = (KᵀSε⁻¹K + Sa⁻¹)⁻¹KᵀSε⁻¹K; DOFS = tr(A).

7 · Ångström exponent and AOD vs wavelength (Ch. 6)

α = ln(τ₁/τ₂)/ln(λ₂/λ₁); τ(λ) = τ₁(λ/λ₁)−α. Defaults: 0.60 at 440 nm and 0.15 at 870 nm.

8 · Size parameter and scattering regime (Ch. 7)

x = 2πr/λ. Try r = 0.0002 µm (N₂), 0.2 µm (sulfate), 5 µm (dust) at λ = 0.55 µm.

9 · Brightness-temperature depression vs dust-layer height (Ch. 8)

I = B(Ts)e−τ + B(Tlayer)(1 − e−τ) at 10 µm; Tlayer = Ts − lapse × z. Simple model: no scattering, no water vapour.
